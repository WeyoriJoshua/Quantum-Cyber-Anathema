## Principal scientific claim

The manuscript does not claim universal quantum or hybrid superiority. It tests whether the relative and complementary utility of Classical and Quantum intrusion-detection branches remains stationary across standard, prospective, and zero-day-style distribution shifts. Classical–Quantum Complementarity Shift (CQCS) formalizes the development-to-target change in conditional quantum rescue, and the empirical results demonstrate both expansion and contraction depending on the attack regime.
